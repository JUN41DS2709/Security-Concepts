# Phishing

> Phishing is primarily a type of social engineering attack where an attacker tries to manipulate the victim by impersonating a trusted entity. It can be anything — someone closer to you, your company where you work, your bank where your account is, or anything regarding your day-to-day life. The attacker takes advantage of this trust and manipulates the victim to reveal sensitive information, such as login credentials, bank details, etc., and can make the victim take an action, for example, clicking links, installing malware, or giving the attacker access to their system.

In short, in a phishing attack, attackers use social engineering tactics to manipulate and make the victim reveal sensitive information or make them perform an action that benefits the attacker.

---

## Types of Phishing

### Vishing

Vishing is where phishing is done through voice calls or phone calls.

**Example:**

An attacker impersonates your company's IT department and asks you for your ID and password for fixing an issue.

---

### Smishing

Smishing is where a phishing attack is done through SMS/text messages.

**Example:**

An attacker sends a message which is too good to be true, something like:

> "Congratulations! You won a lottery of $1 million."

The message may contain a malicious link that tries to steal the victim's information.

---

### Quishing

Quishing is where phishing is done through QR codes.

**Example:**

An attacker creates a malicious QR code that redirects the victim to a phishing website, malicious download, or another attacker-controlled resource.

---

# Types of Phishing Based on Target

### Spear Phishing

This type of phishing is targeting a specific individual or a specific organization.

Unlike normal phishing, spear phishing is usually more targeted and customized toward the victim.

---

### Whaling

Whaling is a type of phishing that targets high-profile individuals, such as CEOs, managers, executives, or other senior-level employees.

It is essentially a highly targeted form of phishing aimed at individuals who have access to valuable information or resources.
